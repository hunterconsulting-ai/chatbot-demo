# About Acme Corp

## Who We Are

Acme Corp is a family-owned home goods retailer founded in 1987 in Columbus, Ohio. We specialize in kitchen appliances, cookware, and home organization products. With over 35 years in business, we have served more than 500,000 customers across the United States.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday home life simpler, better organized, and more enjoyable through quality products and exceptional customer service.

## Our Team

Acme Corp employs approximately 120 people across our Columbus headquarters, regional warehouse in Indianapolis, and our customer service center in Cincinnati. Our customer service team is available Monday through Friday, 8am to 7pm Eastern Time, and Saturday 9am to 5pm Eastern Time.
