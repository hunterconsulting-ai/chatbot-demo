# Acme Corp Pricing & Payment

## Price Match Policy

Acme Corp will match the current advertised price of any identical item sold by a U.S.-based authorized retailer. To request a price match:
1. Contact us before completing your purchase
2. Provide a link or screenshot showing the competitor's current price
3. The item must be in stock at the competitor at the time of the request
4. Price matches are applied as a discount at checkout — not as a post-purchase credit

We do not price match marketplace sellers (Amazon third-party, eBay), warehouse clubs (Costco, Sam's Club), or liquidation retailers.

## Sales Tax

Sales tax is collected on all orders shipped to states where Acme Corp has nexus. Tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address.

## Accepted Payment Methods

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal and PayPal Pay Later
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Acme Corp Gift Cards
- Acme Corp Store Credit

We do not accept checks, money orders, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency.

## Acme Rewards Program

Acme Rewards members earn 1 point per $1 spent. Points are redeemable at a rate of 100 points = $1 off a future purchase. Points expire 12 months after the transaction date. Membership is free — sign up at checkout or on our website.

**Bonus Point Events:**
- Double points on all orders in November
- Triple points on your birthday month (register birthdate in account settings)
- 500 bonus points for writing a verified product review

## Financing

We offer 0% APR financing for 12 months on orders of $299 or more through our partner Acme Financial (subject to credit approval). Apply at checkout.
