# Acme Corp Returns & Exchanges

## Return Window

Most items may be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. The following exceptions apply:

- **Opened cookware sets:** 30-day return window
- **Small appliances (opened):** 30-day return window
- **Personalized or custom items:** Non-returnable
- **Gift cards:** Non-returnable and non-refundable

## Condition Requirements

Items must be returned in their original condition with all original packaging, accessories, and documentation. We reserve the right to apply a 15% restocking fee or refuse returns on items that are:
- Damaged due to misuse or negligence
- Missing original accessories or parts
- Returned without original packaging

## How to Start a Return

1. Log in to your Acme Corp account and go to Order History
2. Select the item you wish to return and click "Start Return"
3. Print your prepaid return shipping label (standard returns are free)
4. Drop the package at any UPS location within 7 days of label generation

If you do not have an account, contact customer service at 1-800-555-2674 or support@acmecorp.example.com with your order number.

## Refund Timeline

- Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of receiving your return at our warehouse
- Credit card refunds take an additional 3–7 business days to appear on your statement
- PayPal refunds are typically instant once processed
- Acme Rewards points earned on returned items will be deducted from your balance

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item, return the original and place a new order. If you need the replacement urgently, place the new order first — your return refund will follow once we receive the original item.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will arrange a free return and send a replacement at no charge, or issue a full refund including original shipping if a replacement is unavailable.
