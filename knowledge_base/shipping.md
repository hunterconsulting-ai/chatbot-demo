# Acme Corp Shipping

## Shipping Options & Rates

| Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days | $5.99 |
| Expedited Shipping | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight Shipping | Next business day | $24.99 |
| Free Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days | Free on orders $75+ |

Shipping estimates begin from the date your order ships, not the order date. Orders placed before 1pm ET Monday–Friday are typically processed same day.

## Free Shipping

Free standard shipping applies automatically to all orders of $75 or more (after discounts, before tax). No coupon code required. Free shipping is available to all 50 U.S. states.

## Where We Ship

We ship to all 50 U.S. states, including Alaska and Hawaii (standard and expedited only; overnight not available for AK/HI). We do not currently ship to U.S. territories, APO/FPO addresses, or international destinations.

## Order Tracking

A shipping confirmation email with a tracking link is sent as soon as your order ships. You can also track your order in your Acme Corp account under Order History. We ship via UPS and USPS depending on your location and selected shipping method.

## Large Item Shipping

Items weighing over 50 lbs or classified as oversized (e.g., large cookware sets, storage rack systems) ship via freight carrier. Freight orders require a delivery appointment — our carrier will call to schedule. Freight delivery does not include inside delivery or setup; items are delivered to the first dry area of your home.

## Order Changes & Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled or modified within 1 hour of placement by contacting customer service. Once an order has been picked and packed, we cannot guarantee changes can be made.

## Holiday Shipping

During peak seasons (Thanksgiving week through New Year's), processing times may be extended by 1–2 business days. Order early to ensure holiday delivery. Guaranteed holiday delivery cutoff dates are posted on our website each November.
