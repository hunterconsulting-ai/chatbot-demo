# Acme Corp — Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**How do I check my order status?**
Log in to your account and go to Order History. You'll see the current status of every order and a tracking link once your order has shipped. You can also check using the tracking number in your shipping confirmation email.

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
Orders can be modified within 1 hour of placement. Contact us at 1-800-555-2674 as soon as possible. Once your order is picked and packed, changes may not be possible.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes. Gift wrapping is available for $4.99 per item and can be selected during checkout. A personalized gift message (up to 200 characters) can be included at no additional charge.

**Can I place an order by phone?**
Yes. Our customer service team can place orders by phone Monday–Friday 8am–7pm ET and Saturday 9am–5pm ET at 1-800-555-2674.

## Products & Warranty

**What warranty do your products carry?**
Most kitchen appliances carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Select premium models include a 3-year warranty — check the individual product page. Our 10-Piece Stainless Pro Cookware Set includes a lifetime warranty against defects.

**Do you sell replacement parts?**
Yes, replacement parts are available for most major appliances we carry. Search by model number on our website or contact customer service with your model number and we will locate the correct part.

**Are your non-stick products PFOA-free?**
Yes. All non-stick cookware and bakeware sold by Acme Corp uses PFOA-free coatings. Product pages display full material certifications.

## Account & Privacy

**Do I need an account to place an order?**
No. We offer guest checkout. However, creating an account gives you order history access, faster checkout, access to the Acme Rewards program, and easier returns.

**How do I reset my password?**
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. A reset link will be sent within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

**Do you sell my personal information?**
No. Acme Corp does not sell, rent, or share customer personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy at acmecorp.example.com/privacy for full details.

## Gift Cards

**How do I check my gift card balance?**
Visit acmecorp.example.com/giftcard and enter your gift card number and PIN, or contact customer service.

**Do gift cards expire?**
No. Acme Corp gift cards do not expire and have no inactivity fees.

**Can I use multiple gift cards on one order?**
Yes. Up to 5 gift cards can be applied to a single order.
